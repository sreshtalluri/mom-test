## Founder's likely assumption
Independent cafés lose time and money to disorganized supplier ordering.

## Real top problem
Supplier ordering. Says so immediately when asked about the hardest part of the week.

## Does this person have the assumed problem?
Yes, and they know it. Decision-maker: yes, it's their café.

## Incidents
- Two Saturdays ago: oat milk didn't arrive. Drove to the wholesale club mid-rush, one hour gone, paid retail (about $60 over).
- Last month: double-ordered pastry flour because two people texted the same supplier. $140 of flour they didn't need.
- Roughly every two weeks something is wrong: quantity, day, or item.

## Current solution
Texts, WhatsApp, one supplier who insists on phone calls. A notebook by the register. Works until it doesn't.

## Past attempts
Tried a shared Google Sheet for six weeks. Staff stopped updating it. "It's one more thing on a Saturday."

## Spending
Nothing on software for this. Pays a bookkeeper $300/month who occasionally flags duplicate invoices after the fact. Would put "a couple hundred a month" on the card without thinking if it actually worked.

## Decision path
Owner decides alone. Last purchase: a new POS, chosen in a week after a friend recommended it.

## Constraint
Staff won't download another app. Whatever it is has to work over text or on the POS tablet.

## Urgency trigger
Opening a second location in three months. "Two of everything."

## Feature they'll volunteer
"Just let me text one number and have it go to the right supplier." Motive: they don't want to change how staff already communicate.

## Emotional nerve
The Saturday drive. "I was standing in a wholesale club aisle while my café had a line out the door."
